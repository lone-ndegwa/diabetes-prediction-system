Recommended Next Steps

The assessment indicates a higher predicted risk of diabetes. The following steps may help guide further evaluation and healthy lifestyle choices.

1. Seek Further Medical Assessment

Arrange an appointment with a qualified healthcare professional for further evaluation. Appropriate diagnostic testing may be recommended based on the patient's individual circumstances.

2. Discuss Blood Glucose Testing

A healthcare professional can determine whether additional blood glucose testing is appropriate and interpret the results in the context of the patient's medical history.

3. Maintain a Healthy Diet

Aim for a balanced diet that includes vegetables, whole grains, lean sources of protein, and other nutrient-rich foods while limiting excessive intake of added sugars and highly processed foods.

4. Stay Physically Active

Regular physical activity can support overall health and help with blood glucose management. Activity should be appropriate for the individual's health and physical condition.

5. Monitor Health Regularly

Continue appropriate health monitoring and discuss any concerning symptoms or changes with a healthcare professional.

Important: This system provides a risk prediction and general recommendations. It does not diagnose diabetes. The prediction should not replace professional medical assessment, diagnostic testing, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.
